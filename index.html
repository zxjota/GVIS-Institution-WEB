Privacy Policy for GVIS Institution

Last Updated: June 1, 2024

GVIS Institution (Global Vision International School) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our students, parents, staff, and all members of our educational community. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information within our educational environment.

This Privacy Policy describes how GVIS Institution ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you interact with our educational services, website, and applications (collectively, the "Services").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe, responsible, and ethical manner, in compliance with applicable educational data privacy laws. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your personal information.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to all students, parents, staff, and visitors of GVIS Institution. By accessing or using our educational services, you agree to the collection, use, and sharing of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy, in accordance with applicable laws including FERPA, COPPA, and GDPR where applicable.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or educational services. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Information We Collect

As an educational institution, we collect several types of information necessary for educational purposes, student safety, and institutional operations:

  • Student Information: Academic records, attendance data, assessment results, and educational progress information.
  • Personal Information: Contact details, identification information, and demographic data for students, parents, and staff.
  • Health and Safety Information: Medical records, emergency contact information, and other data necessary for student welfare.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our educational platforms, including login data, device information, and online activity.
  • Financial Information: Payment information for tuition and other school-related expenses (processed securely through trusted third-party providers).

Educational Data Protection: We adhere to strict educational data privacy standards and do not sell student or family information. Data is only shared as necessary for educational purposes, legal compliance, or with explicit consent.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate educational and institutional purposes, including:

  • Providing and improving educational services and instruction
  • Maintaining student records and academic progress tracking
  • Ensuring student health, safety, and well-being on campus
  • Communicating with parents and guardians about student progress and school activities
  • Processing tuition payments and managing institutional finances
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements for educational institutions
  • Conducting research and analysis to improve educational outcomes (anonymized where possible)

Information Sharing

We may share personal information in the following circumstances, always with appropriate safeguards:

  • Educational Partners: With trusted educational service providers who support our academic programs, under strict data protection agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, regulation, or legal process, particularly relating to educational records and child welfare.
  • Health and Safety: With medical professionals or emergency services when necessary for student welfare.
  • Educational Authorities: With government educational bodies as required for accreditation, reporting, or funding purposes.
  • With Consent: When you have provided explicit consent for specific sharing arrangements.

Data Security

We implement comprehensive technical, administrative, and physical security measures designed to protect educational records and personal information. These measures include encryption, access controls, regular security audits, and staff training on data protection.

As an educational institution, we adhere to industry-standard security frameworks specifically designed for protecting sensitive student data. All staff members with access to student information undergo thorough background checks and privacy training.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and role (student, parent, or staff), you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access and review educational records (as provided under FERPA and similar regulations)
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • The right to limit disclosure of directory information
  • The right to data portability for information you've provided
  • The right to withdraw consent for non-essential processing

To exercise these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@gvis.edu. We will respond to your request in accordance with applicable educational privacy laws.

Children's Privacy

As an educational institution serving minors, we are particularly committed to protecting children's privacy. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

For students under 13, we collect and process personal information only with parental consent or as permitted by educational laws. Parents have the right to review their child's information, request deletion, and refuse further collection or use of their child's information.

Parental Rights: Parents and guardians may review their child's educational records, consent to specific data uses, or withdraw consent by contacting our Student Records Office at records@gvis.edu.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our educational practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify the school community of significant changes through our usual communication channels (email, parent portal, and announcements).

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued engagement with GVIS Institution after we post any modifications constitutes your acknowledgment of the changes and your agreement to abide by the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our Data Protection Team:

Data Protection Officer: dpo@gvis.edu
